Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common account questions.

Where can I see my orders?

Open My Orders. Your order history appears when the authenticated orders API is connected.

How do I change my delivery address?

Open Saved Addresses to add, edit or remove an address saved in this browser. Account-wide syncing requires the backend API.

How do I reset my password?

Use the secure password-reset process provided by your account authentication backend. Never share your password with support.
